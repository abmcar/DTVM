\section{Conclusion}

This paper builds a two-tier peephole rewrite system for the
deterministic EVM JIT: the 70 dMIR rules pass the Z3 bit-vector
equivalence check, and the 13 x86 rules are covered by paired
differential runs. A small intersection with Albert et
al.~\cite{verified_evm_cav23} on bytecode-level identities is
complementary; the methodology---first-class carry/overflow operators
in a domain-specific IR so that automatic equivalence checking covers
a larger surface---generalizes to other deterministic execution
environments with multi-word arithmetic (Wasm big-integer, zk-VM
back-ends). Future work: a formal x86 model, dataflow-based pruning to
scale beyond the 853-candidate capacity run and the 1966-rule coverage
run reported here, and an orthogonal-composition study with
bytecode-layer rule sets to quantify residual speedup.
