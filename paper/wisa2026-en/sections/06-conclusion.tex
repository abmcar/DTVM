\section{Conclusion}

This paper builds a two-tier peephole rewrite system for the
deterministic EVM JIT: 70 dMIR rules pass Z3 bit-vector equivalence
checks, and 13 x86 rules are covered by paired differential runs. A
small intersection with Albert et al.~\cite{verified_evm_cav23} on
bytecode-level identities is complementary; first-class carry/overflow
operators in a domain-specific IR let automatic equivalence checking
cover a larger surface and generalize to other deterministic execution
environments with multi-word arithmetic (Wasm big-integer, zk-VM
back-ends). Future work includes a formal x86 model, dataflow-based
pruning beyond the 853-candidate capacity run and the 1966-rule coverage
run, and an orthogonal-composition study quantifying the additional
speedup from layering bytecode-level rules on top of ours.
