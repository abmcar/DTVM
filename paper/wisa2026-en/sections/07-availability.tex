\section*{Data and Code Availability}

Rule synthesis scripts, rule sets (JSON + Z3 queries), evaluation
scripts, and data are in an anonymous mirror named in the double-blind
materials and de-anonymized upon acceptance. CI re-runs every rule's
equivalence check on change.
