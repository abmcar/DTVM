\section{Conclusion}

This paper extends a domain-specific JIT IR (dMIR) with first-class
carry and overflow operators and builds an SMT-checked peephole layer
on top of it: 70 dMIR rules pass Z3 admission over arithmetic and
flags, and 13 x86 cleanup rules are covered by matched differential
testing. The combination delivers a +7.24\% geometric-mean speedup on
evmone-bench while preserving byte-identical final state across
5{,}884 Cancun differential tests, with correctness bounded by suite
coverage.

Beyond the peephole layer itself, we add a measurement pipeline---a
shadow audit that pairs a baseline matcher with a recursive
commutative-aware matcher at every commutative-rooted subtree, and a
strict statistical validation harness that turns matcher-capability
claims into bench-paired CI-bounded tests---which on the existing
70-rule production set reports no statistically valid recovery
($n_{\text{picks}}=0$): the contribution is the methodology and the
negative result, not a rule yield. The recursive matcher infrastructure
lands prod-build-clean. Future work includes a formal x86 model to
replace the differential-test fallback at the cleanup layer,
cross-engine validation against evmone and revm, and running the
validation pipeline against an extended rule set drawn from per-rule
attribution analysis on production traces.
